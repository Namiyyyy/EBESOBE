more on us

ebe sobe kollektiv is a collective for urban phenomenological experimentation. Through workshops and participarty practices, it creates a sensory index of urban space.

At this phase of the project we focus on playgrounds, and our target group is children between 8-12. As we grow, we would like to test our practice on other architectural typologies of play.

We are looking for venues we can recreate our format, make more workshops and reach more children. If you have a venue, or an event format that we can collaborate, please don't hesitate to reach us!
